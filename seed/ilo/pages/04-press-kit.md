## Short bio

Ilo Odena makes objects, writes, and keeps moments.

## Long bio

Ilo Odena works across small objects, writing and what she calls moments: a few seconds of a place, kept whole. She grew up in the wetlands, worked a market stall, and spent three summers at a lake before making Hollow, a palm-sized stone that holds one drop of lake water. She made it with Y3K Club. She lives by the lake and writes a letter a few times a year.

## Fast facts

- Makes small objects, one at a time
- Best-known piece: Hollow, a stone for listening to a lake
- Writes a few letters a year
- Lives by the lake
