# Darmio trademark policy

> **Draft: needs a lawyer's read before release.** Written in plain words by
> a non-lawyer. Nothing here has been checked against trademark law, and the
> name is not yet registered.

The code is MIT. The name is not.

**The code** (everything in this repo) is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.
Run it, change it, host it, sell what you make with it. You don't need to ask.

**The name "Darmio"** and its logo belong to the maintainer of this project
(the legal owner's name goes here before release: `[OWNER NAME / ENTITY]`).
Anyone may host the code. Nobody else gets to be Darmio.

## Fine, no need to ask

- Saying you use Darmio, built your site with it, or run it for clients.
  "Built with Darmio" and "Powered by Darmio" are fine.
- Writing about Darmio, reviewing it, teaching it, making videos about it.
- Linking to this repo, and saying that your project is compatible with
  Darmio.
- Keeping the name and credits that ship in your copy of the code, and
  forking it to fix or change things for yourself.
- Hosting the unmodified code for yourself or for the clients whose sites
  you run, while being clear it's your service.

## Ask first

- Naming a product, service, company, domain or social account with
  "Darmio" in it, or something close enough to be confused with it.
- Offering a hosted service that could look like it comes from the Darmio
  project.
- Selling themes, plugins or templates under a name that starts with or
  includes "Darmio". (Saying "for Darmio" is fine.)
- Using the logo, or anything that suggests the project endorses you.

## Not allowed

- Calling your fork, product or service "Darmio", or presenting it as the
  official one.
- Implying the project made, approved or supports something it didn't.

## If you fork it

Rename it. Keep the MIT licence text and copyright line. You can say it
"is a fork of Darmio" in plain text.

## Asking permission

Email `[CONTACT EMAIL]` with what you're making and where the name would
appear. Permission, if given, is in writing and can be withdrawn for misuse.

This policy can change. Changes don't reach back to what was fine before.
