---
title: Answering slowly
categories: habits
date: 2025-08-21
description: I read everything. I answer about a week later, and the answers are better for it.
---

I read every message I get. I answer most of them about a week later.

It started as a failure. I was behind, always, and every reply felt like a debt. Then I noticed the late replies were better. A week gave the question time to settle, and gave me time to find out what I actually thought.

Now I do it on purpose. Messages come in during the week and wait. On Sunday mornings I answer them, by hand first on paper, then typed. It takes about two hours. Nobody has ever been upset that I took a week. A few people have said it was the first reply in a while that felt like a letter.

Fast is right for some things: a lift to the station, a door left open. For anything that matters, slow is kinder. To the other person, and to the answer.
