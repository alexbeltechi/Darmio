## Who is Ilo Odena?

She makes small objects, writes, and keeps moments. Her best-known piece is Hollow, a stone that holds one drop of lake water.

## What is a moment?

A few seconds of a place, kept whole: the light, the temperature, the sound underneath, what was in the air. Her glasses keep them. She keeps about one a week.

## Do the glasses keep people?

No. She keeps places, and her own hands in them. Never faces.

## Can I buy a Hollow?

Not from a shop. She makes two a year, each for a particular lake, river or shore. See [Work with me](/work-with-me).

## Where does she live?

By the lake where Hollow was made.

## How do I hear from her?

The [newsletter](/#newsletter): a few letters a year. She also answers email, slowly.
