## Home

A page built from blocks, at `home`, so it overrides the feed. Removing the
examples removes it, and the feed is back. Blocks marked *new* don't exist
yet.

1. **Intro** *(new)*: the portrait loop (`ilo.mp4`, poster `ilo.jpg`),
   square, then:

   > Ilo Odena makes objects, writes, and keeps moments.
   >
   > Her work starts with listening: to a lake, a market at closing time, a
   > room at four in the morning. What she keeps, she keeps whole: the light,
   > the temperature, the sound under the sound.
   >
   > She writes here a few times a year about making things slowly and
   > finishing them anyway. [More about Ilo](/about).

2. **Writing** *(new: latest posts, as a list)*: category `writing`, every
   post, each as title, year and read time; "All N posts" when there are
   more than seven.

3. **Moments** *(new: latest posts, as a strip)*: category `moments`, the
   last six, loops playing muted; "All moments" links to `/moments`.

4. **About** *(new: link list)*: About Ilo (`/about`), Hollow (`/about#hollow`).

5. **Elsewhere** *(link list)*: Y3K Club (`https://y3k.club`), Newsletter
