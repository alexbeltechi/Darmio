Ilo Odena is a maker from the year 3000. She makes small objects, writes, and keeps moments, and doesn't separate the three much.

:::gallery layout=auto width=auto
![Ilo in a sheer teal gown, walking along a black-sand shore, reflected in still water](file:ilo-black-sand.jpg)
:::

She spent three summers at a lake and made Hollow, a palm-sized stone that holds one drop of lake water. She made it with [Y3K Club](https://y3k.club), people who each make one thing they love, on a deadline, together.

She lives by the lake and answers letters slowly.
