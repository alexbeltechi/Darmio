---
title: Four in the morning
categories: writing
date: 2025-04-08
description: The room is different before anyone needs anything from it.
---

I don't get up at four to be productive. I get up because the room is different then.

Nothing needs anything from it yet. The kettle is the loudest thing for an hour. The light comes in grey and flat, so you see the shape of things before their colour. My hands are slower, and that turns out to be right for most of what I make.

I don't plan these hours. I sit with whatever's on the bench from yesterday and touch it until I know what it wants next. Some mornings that's ten minutes of sanding. Some mornings it's nothing, and I write instead.

By seven the day has started arguing with me. Messages, errands, other people's plans. That's fine. The work that matters already happened while the room was quiet.

If you can't do four, try the first hour of whenever you wake. Before the phone. Just the room, and the thing you're making.
