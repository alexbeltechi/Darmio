---
title: What I bring to the lake
categories: writing
date: 2026-04-30
description: A short list, kept short on purpose.
---

People ask what I take when I go to the lake to work. The list is short, and keeping it short is most of the method.

- **A towel,** for sitting on the stone edge, which is cold until noon.
- **A notebook** with soft pages, and a pencil. Pens die in the damp.
- **Bread,** for me and, against the rules, the ducks.
- **One stone** I'm working on, in a cloth bag.
- **Nothing that makes a sound.** No phone, no music.

I stay until something happens. Usually it's small: the wind changing, a heron landing, the light going silver on the north side. I write down what I noticed, not what I thought about it.

Then I go home. The making happens later, at the bench. But it starts here, with the list kept short and the hours kept long.
