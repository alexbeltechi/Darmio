Ilo Odena works across objects, writing and moments, and doesn't separate them much. A stone she made last autumn started as a note, and the note started as a morning at the lake that she kept.

She wears the same glasses most days. They're older than they look, and they remember more than she does. The moments on this site come from them: a few seconds of light, and whatever else was in the air.

## Hollow

:::gallery layout=auto width=auto
![ilo-lake.jpg: Ilo from behind, sitting at the edge of a still lake at sunrise](placeholder)
:::

Last autumn she made Hollow, a palm-sized stone that holds one drop of lake water. You leave it in the shallows overnight; in the morning you hold it, breathe on it until the dish fogs, and think of your question.

She made it in one sprint with [Y3K Club](https://y3k.club), a club of people who each make one thing they love, on a deadline, together.

## Writing to her

She answers email slowly and reads all of it. The quickest way to hear from her is the newsletter: a few letters a year.
