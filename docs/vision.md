# Vision

Where this platform is going and why. Read this before planning any feature;
if a request seems to pull against it, say so.

## What it is

**Turn your practice into a business people take seriously. Built with AI,
always yours.** A publishing platform for creators: makers, solo founders,
coaches, instructors, photographers, designers, artists, anyone launching or
relaunching a practice. It launches the person and their offer first, with
their agent or by hand, through a simple, minimal, clean interface.
Who it sells to, the launch flow, the phases and the competition are in
`positioning.md`, which leads where the two disagree (decisions,
2026-10-29).

Alex is the first customer: beltechi.com moves onto it at the pre-release
(2026-11-25); until then it's on Framer. The CMS is **Darmio** (decisions,
2026-10-26); **Parabola** is its design system.

## Why

- **Free to own.** The whole CMS is open source under MIT. People download
  it, run it on their own accounts with their own agent, adapt it, and
  maybe contribute back. Nothing it needs runs on a server of ours.
- **Yours to leave with.** Your content, settings and the agent's documents
  export as one file that any Darmio imports, whatever version you're on.
  The code you have is yours under MIT, whatever we do later.
- **Three Darmios** (decisions, 2026-10-28): the self-hosted one, free; a
  web version, later, where we host it and our agent works on it, paid,
  for makers who want to start without a terminal; and themes, ours and
  other designers', that work on both. Paid is what costs us money or
  time; code is never paid, and no feature moves from free to paid.
- **Bring people to your own site** in as free a format as possible.

## Who it's for

Creators who trust their agent: first coaches, instructors and creators;
not SMBs or larger businesses (`positioning.md`). Until the web version,
they're technical, or willing to set up with their own AI reading the docs;
after it, nobody should need to read code. The platform has to carry its own
context: how to upload, how to curate, how to build a page, as optional
skills any agent can read and act on.

## Principles

- **Agent first, UI as backup.** Everything an agent can do, a person can do
  by hand in the admin, without tokens.
- **High craft.** Aesthetics, simplicity, cleanness. Not heavy animation:
  taste in type, spacing and layout.
- **Range.** Product case studies and paintings or photographs must both look
  right. Styles flex; the system holds.
- **Lean.** Minimal dependencies, nothing technically intimidating.
- **Never stale.** The design language and the agent flows must keep evolving.
  Structure things so they can change.
- **No slop.** Pages come from a design process that asks what the page is for
  before drawing anything, never from a one-shot "make me a page".
- **Simple out of the box, grows as you use it.** A fresh install is posts
  and a feed. Navigation, pages and settings appear as you (or your agent)
  make them. Every brand you run lives in the same admin, each on its own
  database (decisions, 2026-10-24).

## In 6–12 months

- It's live, people besides Alex use it, and the base functions work.
- Alex posts easily and sees analytics: who comes in from LinkedIn or X.
- Alex's archive (design, photography, art) is published, and he can
  experience his own body of work.
- The agent is a clever curator: given everything Alex has made, it proposes
  how to present it (case studies, series, collections) without him writing
  each one by hand.

## Later

- **Post as email.** One piece of writing goes to the site, to subscribers,
  or both.
- **The web version.** Darmio hosted by us, our agent inside, for makers
  who want the comfort, with a self-hosted copy of their exact site one
  click away. Not before the open-source release, and not announced with it.
- **Marketing, outreach and email,** the way Polsia covers them, in both
  versions: the site, the list, the posts to X and LinkedIn, the follow-ups,
  one agent across all of it.
- **A planner,** Linear-like, for makers' own projects.

## Not this

- A stale CMS that feels old.
- A tool that needs a developer to run or understand.
- A free version with features held back for a paid one. Hosting, our
  agent and designs are sold; features never are.
- A place you can't leave. Export is always there, never behind a paywall.
- A tool locked to one agent. Any agent that speaks MCP works on it.
