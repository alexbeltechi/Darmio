## A stone for your water

I make two Hollows a year, each for one lake, river or shore that matters to someone. I spend a season there first, listening, before I cut the stone. You get the stone, the notes from that season, and one moment I kept there.

## Listening walks

A morning at your lake, park or field with up to eight people. No phones. We walk slowly, stop often, and each person leaves with one thing they noticed and wrote down. For teams, schools and friends.

## The small version

Four sessions for a maker stuck on something too big. We cut it to one thing, one person who'll hold it, and one day it's finished. Then you make it.

## Talks and writing

Short talks on listening and on finishing things, and essays for magazines that like slow subjects.
