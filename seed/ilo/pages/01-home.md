Ilo Odena makes objects, writes, and keeps moments.

Her work starts with listening: to a lake, a market at closing time, a room at four in the morning. Her best-known piece is Hollow, a stone that holds one drop of lake water and tells you what the night was like. She takes a few [commissions](/work-with-me) a year.

She writes here a few times a year about making things slowly and finishing them anyway. [More about Ilo.](/about)
